# Choosing a Co-Op Game for a Beginner (Player 2 Guide)

Introducing someone who rarely plays games to cooperative gaming can be wonderful—or deeply frustrating—depending on the game you choose.

If your Player 2 is still getting comfortable holding a controller, steering a 3D camera while walking, or reacting under time pressure, follow these golden rules:

## 1. Eliminate Camera Struggle
Moving an avatar with the left stick while adjusting a 3D camera with the right stick is second nature to lifelong gamers, but completely disorienting to beginners.
- **Start with fixed-camera 2D or isometric games:** *Super Mario 3D World*, *Snipperclips*, *Unravel Two*, *Cat Quest II*, or *Lovers in a Dangerous Spacetime*.

## 2. Pick Games with Asymmetrical Roles or Infinite Respawn
In high-punishment games, one player dying forces a restart for both, creating anxiety.
- Look for games where **death isn't permanent**: in *Kirby and the Forgotten Land*, *Kirby's Return to Dream Land Deluxe*, or *Super Mario Bros. Wonder*, a fallen partner floats in a bubble or respawns seamlessly next to Player 1.
- In *Super Mario Odyssey*, Player 2 can control Cappy, floating freely and collecting coins with zero pressure of falling down pits.

## 3. Prioritize Cooperation Over Competition
Games like *Overcooked!* are beloved, but their high-pressure timers and kitchen fires can cause real-life arguments. For couples or relaxed evenings:
- Choose low-stress vibes like **Very Chill** or **Cozy**: *Haven*, *Blanc*, *Captain Toad: Treasure Tracker*, or *Untitled Goose Game*.
