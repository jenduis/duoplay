# Nintendo Switch 2: GameShare & Multiplayer Features

As Nintendo prepares its next-generation console (codenamed / commonly referred to as **Nintendo Switch 2**), cooperative and multiplayer gaming continues to be a cornerstone of the platform.

## What is GameShare?

Similar to the legendary **Download Play** feature on Nintendo 3DS and DS, **GameShare** refers to hardware and platform features that allow one console hosting a game to beam multiplayer sessions or guest passes to nearby consoles.

On DuoPlay:
- Games verified or confirmed with GameShare capabilities are flagged with `gameShare: true`.
- Switch 2 games also maintain full backwards compatibility with Nintendo Switch multiplayer titles.

## Backwards Compatibility for Switch Co-Op

- Almost all existing Nintendo Switch local multiplayer and split-screen games run seamlessly on the new hardware.
- Performance enhancements: titles with dynamic resolution scaling or unlocked frame rates experience smoother split-screen performance.
