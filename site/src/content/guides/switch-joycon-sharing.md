# Nintendo Switch: Joy-Con Sharing Guide

One of the best hardware features of the Nintendo Switch is that every console comes out of the box with two controllers: the **Left** and **Right Joy-Cons**.

## How to Set Up 2-Player Joy-Con Sharing

1. **Detach Both Joy-Cons** from the Switch console or Joy-Con Grip.
2. From the HOME menu, select the **Controllers** icon (circle with Joy-Con silhouette).
3. Select **Change Grip / Order**.
4. Turn each Joy-Con horizontally sideways.
5. Press the **SL and SR buttons** simultaneously along the flat inner rail on each Joy-Con until the controller lights stop blinking.
6. Press the **(A)** or **right face button** on the horizontal Joy-Con to confirm.
