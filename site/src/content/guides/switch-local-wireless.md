# Nintendo Switch Local Wireless Guide

Local Wireless (often called Local Play or Ad-Hoc Play) allows 2 to 8 Nintendo Switch consoles to communicate directly with one another using their built-in Wi-Fi chips—**no internet connection or Wi-Fi access point required**.

## How Local Wireless Works

- Each player needs their own Nintendo Switch console and their own copy of the game (physical cartridge or digital download).
- The host console creates a direct, peer-to-peer Wi-Fi network.
- Other consoles within approximately 30 feet (10 meters) discover the lobby and join directly.

## Step-by-Step Setup

1. **Keep Both Consoles Nearby:** Sit in the same room or within 10 meters of each other.
2. **Launch the Game:** Both players launch the game on their respective Switch consoles.
3. **Select Wireless Play:** From the game's main menu, select **Local Wireless**, **Wireless Play**, or **Nearby Players** (do not select *Online Play*).
4. **Host Creates Room:** Player 1 selects **Create Room** or **Host Session**.
5. **Guest Joins Room:** Player 2 selects **Search for Room** or taps Player 1's avatar when it appears in the lobby list.

## Top Nintendo Switch Local Wireless Co-Op Games

- **Monster Hunter Rise** — 4-player hunting parties on separate screens.
- **Animal Crossing: New Horizons** — Island visits and sharing over local wireless.
- **Splatoon 3 (The Shoal)** — 4-player Salmon Run co-op sessions.
- **Mario Kart 8 Deluxe** — Up to 8 wireless consoles racing together.
