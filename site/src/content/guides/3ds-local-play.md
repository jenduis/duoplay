# Nintendo 3DS & DS Multi-Card Local Play

While Download Play lets you share with one game card, **Multi-Card Local Play** provides the definitive multiplayer experience on Nintendo 3DS and original Nintendo DS: all game content, custom save files, full character rosters, and no wireless streaming lag.

## How to Connect via Multi-Card Local Play

1. **Check Compatibility:** Ensure both players have the same game cartridge inserted (or digital copy installed). *Note: Regional game version differences can occasionally prevent local matchmaking.*
2. **Enable Wireless Switch:** On original 3DS / 3DS XL consoles, ensure the physical wireless slider on the right side is switched ON (yellow LED illuminated). On New 3DS / 2DS, verify Wi-Fi is enabled in the HOME menu top-left settings.
3. **Launch the Game on Both Consoles:** Start the game and head to the multiplayer menu.
4. **Select Multi-Card Play:** Choose **Local Play** or **Multi-Card Wireless**.
5. **Host and Join:** One player creates a group, and the other player connects to the group lobby.
