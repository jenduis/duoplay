# Nintendo 3DS: 1-Cartridge Download Play Guide

**Download Play** is one of the most generous multiplayer features in handheld gaming history: **only one person needs to own the game cartridge**, and up to 7 other friends with Nintendo 3DS or 2DS consoles can join completely free over direct local wireless.

## How to Connect via Download Play

### 1. Host Console (Owns the Game Card)
- Insert the game cartridge and launch the game.
- In the main menu, navigate to **Multiplayer** or **Wireless Play**.
- Select **Download Play (Host)** or **Create Session**.

### 2. Guest Consoles (No Game Card Required)
- Turn on your Nintendo 3DS / 2DS console.
- From the HOME Menu, open the orange **Download Play** app (icon with two handheld screens).
- Tap **Nintendo 3DS** (or **Nintendo DS** if playing a DS game).
- Select the host's game name when it appears on screen, and confirm **Download**.
- The temporary multiplayer client will download wirelessly, and the match will start!

## Verified Must-Play Download Play Titles
- **Mario Kart 7** — Full 8-player Grand Prix & VS racing.
- **The Legend of Zelda: Tri Force Heroes** — 3-player cooperative campaign.
- **Luigi's Mansion: Dark Moon** — 4-player ScareScraper co-op tower climbing.
- **Kirby: Planet Robobot** — 4-player Team Kirby Clash boss battles.
