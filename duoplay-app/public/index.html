💡 Console Multiplayer Reference & Setup Guide

Nintendo Switch: 1-Console Joy-Con Sharing vs. Local Wireless

The Switch offers both single-console couch play and multi-console local wireless:

  • Single Console (Shared / Split Screen): Games like It Takes Two, Snipperclips, Overcooked, Blanc, and Stardew Valley can be played on 1 Switch simply by detaching Joy-Cons or connecting extra controllers.
  • Local Wireless (Multiple Switches): Games like Splatoon 3, Monster Hunter Rise, and Pokemon communicate directly via ad-hoc local Wi-Fi without needing an internet connection.
Nintendo 3DS / DS: 1-Cartridge Download Play vs. Multi-Card Wireless

Download Play allows two or more 3DS/DS systems to play together wirelessly using only a single game cartridge:

  • Host: Inserts the cartridge and hosts a "Download Play" session.
  • Guest: Opens the orange "Download Play" icon on the 3DS Home Menu to connect.
  • Top Single-Cart Games: Mario Kart 7 (8 players), Luigi's Mansion (Gooigi co-op), Zelda: Tri Force Heroes (3-player campaign), and Rhythm Heaven Megamix.
PC, PlayStation & Xbox: Seamless Couch & LAN Co-Op

Many modern titles support full multi-controller play on PC (using Steam Input with any controller) or split-screen couch co-op on PS4/PS5 and Xbox Series X/S. Furthermore, community tools like Nucleus Co-Op allow split-screen play on PC for hundreds of traditionally online-only games.

Unofficial Multiplayer Mods & Source Ports (Megalist Reference)

Community decompilations and multiplayer mods (from the Unofficial Multiplayer Mods Megalist) allow classic single-player games to be enjoyed in co-op:

  • Super Mario 64 Coop DX (sm64ex-coop): Full campaign 2-player co-op running at 60 FPS natively on PC and hacked Switch consoles with synced stars and custom characters.
  • The Legend of Zelda (Ship of Harkinian): Native source ports with widescreen 60 FPS and multiplayer crowd-control.
  • Sonic 3 A.I.R.: 16-bit widescreen remaster running on Switch and PC with Sonic & Tails co-op.
  • Undertale Together & Skyrim Together: Full community cooperative campaign mods on PC.
